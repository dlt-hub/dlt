# Security and compliance

At dltHub, we take the security, privacy, and protection of customer data seriously. We are committed to maintaining a secure, reliable, and transparent environment for our customers, partners, and stakeholders.

Our security and compliance practices are designed to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of our systems and information, reduce operational risks, and maintain appropriate safeguards across our organization and technology infrastructure.

We continuously review and improve our security practices to align with industry standards and best practices.

The dltHub platform is operated by ScaleVector GmbH, which is the legal entity covered by our independent SOC 2 audits.

## Security program

Our security program focuses on protecting company systems, infrastructure, and customer information. Key areas of our security program include:

- **Access control**: access to company systems and sensitive information is restricted to authorized individuals based on business needs and responsibilities.
- **Data protection**: appropriate safeguards are implemented to protect sensitive and customer information.
- **Encryption**: encryption mechanisms are used to protect data in transit and, where applicable, at rest.
- **Vulnerability management**: security vulnerabilities are assessed and addressed to reduce potential risks.
- **Monitoring**: relevant systems and infrastructure are monitored to identify and respond to potential security incidents.
- **Employee security**: employees are expected to follow established security policies and practices designed to protect company and customer information.

## SOC 2 compliance

These independent audits provide assurance about the design and implementation of security controls and, for SOC 2 Type 2, their operating effectiveness over an audited period.

### Understanding SOC 2 Type 1 and Type 2

SOC 2 (System and Organization Controls 2) is an auditing framework developed by the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants (AICPA). It evaluates an organization's controls against the AICPA Trust Services Criteria.

- **SOC 2 Type 1**: evaluates whether an organization's controls are appropriately designed at a specific point in time.
- **SOC 2 Type 2**: evaluates both the design and operating effectiveness of those controls over an extended period.

A Type 2 report provides additional assurance because it examines how controls operate over time, rather than assessing their design at a single point in time.

### Trust Services Criteria

The SOC 2 framework is based on five Trust Services Criteria:

1. **Security**: protection of systems and information against unauthorized access.
2. **Availability**: ensuring systems remain available and operational as committed.
3. **Processing Integrity**: ensuring system processing is complete, accurate, timely, and authorized.
4. **Confidentiality**: protecting information designated as confidential.
5. **Privacy**: managing personal information in accordance with applicable privacy commitments.

The specific criteria included in the dltHub audit scope are documented in the SOC 2 reports.

## Data privacy and protection

We understand the importance of protecting customer and business information. Our data protection practices are designed to ensure that information is handled responsibly and accessed only by authorized personnel.

Our approach focuses on:

- Limiting access to sensitive information.
- Protecting information against unauthorized access, disclosure, or modification.
- Applying appropriate data retention and disposal practices.
- Handling personal information in accordance with applicable privacy requirements.
- Maintaining safeguards to protect the confidentiality and integrity of customer data.

## Risk management

dltHub takes a proactive approach to identifying, assessing, and managing security and operational risks. Our risk management approach includes:

- Identifying potential security and operational risks.
- Evaluating risks based on their likelihood and potential impact.
- Implementing appropriate mitigation measures.
- Reviewing and updating risk assessments periodically.
- Continuously improving risk management practices as our systems and business evolve.

## Incident response

We maintain processes for identifying, investigating, and responding to potential security incidents. Our incident response approach is designed to:

- Detect and assess potential security incidents.
- Contain and mitigate identified threats.
- Investigate incidents and determine their potential impact.
- Implement corrective actions where necessary.
- Communicate with affected stakeholders when required.

These processes support our commitment to protecting customer information and maintaining the security and reliability of our platform.

## Third-party risk management

We recognize that third-party service providers may introduce additional security and operational risks.

dltHub evaluates relevant third-party providers to understand their security practices and assess potential risks associated with their services. We make sure appropriate safeguards are considered when working with external service providers, and we manage third-party risks as part of our broader security program.

## Continuous improvement

Security and compliance are ongoing responsibilities. We regularly review our security practices, policies, and controls to identify opportunities for improvement and maintain alignment with evolving industry standards and security expectations.

Our SOC 2 audits are an important part of this commitment. They provide an independent assessment of our security controls and support continuous improvement across our organization.
